DETECT on LSST alerts. Every LSST alert ALeRCE puts near a lens or a DESI galaxy is screened by DETECT exactly as a TNS report is: is it in a galaxy with a known redshift, how luminous is it, and is it worth a large-telescope spectrum? Alerts without a DESI or Lens candidate are left out. The rest is handed to a person on the review page.

Watch runs

Every hour at hh:25 UTC the ALeRCE LSST stream (SN / AGN / VS) is matched against cat.lens (≤ 10″) and cat.desi (SN, ≤ 30″ and 3 Re, ZWARN = 0), linked to TNS, and every match goes through DETECT (host rule, score, tags, image). Once a day at 14:30 UTC it re-reads the last 1.5 days for objects ALeRCE ingested late. One page per UTC day of the first LSST detection.
