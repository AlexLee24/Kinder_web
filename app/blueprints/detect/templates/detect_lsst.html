LSST alerts near a lens or a DESI galaxy. Every hour the ALeRCE LSST stream is read (stamp classifier SN / AGN / VS; bogus and asteroids skipped), matched against cat.lens (≤ 10″, any class) and cat.desi (≤ 30″ and ≤ 3 half-light radii, SN class only), and linked to TNS by the LSST-AP-DO-<oid> internal name or a TNS object within 1.5″. Objects in TNS also get their LSST light curve refreshed whenever ALeRCE has a new point.
