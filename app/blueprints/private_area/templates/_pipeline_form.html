      Stacking

      Frames of the same filter and night (two or more) become stack_<filter>_<date>.fits, aligned and averaged in ADU per frame with GAIN, read noise and mid-time written for AutoPhOT. A stack is reused while its frames and these settings stay the same.

      Template subtraction

      Own templates: upload one per filter under Images → Add a template. Pan-STARRS covers Dec > −30°, SDSS its footprint, Legacy Survey most of the extragalactic sky. Without subtraction, exported filters are marked “(w/o TS)”.
