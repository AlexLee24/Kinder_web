    All day

    A duty is one week, Monday to Sunday. They get a push at Lulin sunset every night of it (if their phone has push on). A rotation of N people: one week per person, repeating every N weeks.

    Every GREAT Lab member gets it under the bell and as a push. All-day events count from 09:00.

    Changes apply to every occurrence of this repeating event.
