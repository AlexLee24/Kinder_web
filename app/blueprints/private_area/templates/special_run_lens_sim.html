Physics

θE = √(4GM/c² · Dls/(DlDs)) for a point mass and θE = 4π(σ/c)² Dls/Ds for an isothermal galaxy or cluster, so the arc size grows only as √M (point) or σ² and shrinks when the lens sits close to the source. Strong lensing needs a projected density above Σcr = c²Ds/(4πG DlDls).

Lensing conserves surface brightness: arcs are brighter in total because they are bigger, not brighter per arcsec². The SIE mass here is the projected mass inside 100 kpc of the lens centre (M = πσ²R/G). Cosmology: flat ΛCDM, H0 = 70 km/s/Mpc, Ωm = 0.3.
