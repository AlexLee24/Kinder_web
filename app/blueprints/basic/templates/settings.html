Not arriving?
  • iPhone / iPad: push only works in the Home Screen app (iOS 16.4 or later). In Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen, open Kinder from that icon and turn push on there. Check Settings → Notifications → Kinder.
  • Android: allow notifications for Chrome (or the installed Kinder app) in the phone's settings, and turn battery optimisation off for it if pushes come late.
  • Computer: the browser must be allowed to show notifications in the system settings (macOS: System Settings → Notifications → your browser; Windows: Settings → System → Notifications), and Focus / Do Not Disturb must be off. Chrome and Edge only receive while they are running.
